Uppercase editorial micro-label (dates, cities, section numbers, eyebrows) — Inter 500, 11px, 0.26em tracking.

```jsx
<Label muted>Nº 01 · Otoño – Invierno 2026</Label>
<Label rule>02 · Máster</Label>
<Label vertical muted>Más de 15 años formando modelos</Label>
```

Separate items with a middle dot `·`. Use `muted` for metadata, plain for anything actionable.
