Image frame that enforces the photography rule: B/W at rest, colour + 3 % scale on hover, zero radius, gray placeholder when empty.

```jsx
<Photo src={url} ratio="4 / 5" alt="Máster Modelo Profesional" />
<Photo ratio="16 / 9" caption="Fotografía a sangre · sin texto encima" />
```

Text never sits on top of a Photo; put it beside or below.
