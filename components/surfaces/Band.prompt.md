Full-bleed black section. In the "alternado" page rhythm the hero + title, the Modelos rail and the footer sit in a Band; everything else stays white.

```jsx
<Band><h2>Modelos</h2><Button variant="solid">Ver todos</Button></Band>
<Band padded={false}><video …/></Band>
```

Never stack two Bands; at least one white section between them. Photography inside a Band keeps the same B/W-at-rest rule.
