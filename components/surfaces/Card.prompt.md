Flat rectangular card (no radius, border or shadow) for programs, models, news; photography on top, uppercase title.

```jsx
<Card image={null} eyebrow="Madrid · 12 semanas" title="Pasarela" meta="Presencial · Inicio 12 OCT 2026" action={<Button variant="ghost" icon={<Icon name="arrow-right" size={14}/>}>Ver programa</Button>} />
<Card variant="inverse" title="Casting abierto">Barcelona · 18:00 h</Card>
```

On gray sections use `variant="white"`. Use `variant="plain"` for editorial grids where the photo alone is the surface.
